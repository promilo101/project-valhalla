Victory

Fenrir has fallen

The wolf is dead, and the Endless Winter has begun. Claim your victory now, or march into the snow and see how long your warband lasts.

In Endless mode the waves never stop, enemies grow stronger every wave and the bosses come back. The shop opens only when you choose, at most once every 2 minutes of battle.

Field Guide

Bosses

Elite traits

Elites have a coloured aura and a health bar. They are tougher and drop more souls and 1 gold. Each one has one trait. Beasts, bosses and traits appear here the first time you defeat one.

Relics are sold by the Wandering Merchant and granted by the Cursed Altar. Each one appears here the first time you find it. Until then you only get a hint.

The units you buy in the shop, and the synergies they build. Each unit appears here the first time you buy one.

Hero abilities, offered when you start a raid and after every boss. Each one appears here the first time you take it, with all three levels.

Gear for your hero, sold by the Wandering Merchant and dropped by bosses. Rings and necklaces carry a special power. Each type appears here the first time you buy or take one, and the rarities you have owned light up: Common, Rare and Epic.
